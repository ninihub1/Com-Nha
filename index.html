Our Story

A family kitchen, opened up

Cơm Nhà means "home cooking." Every dish on our menu started at a family table before it ever reached a restaurant one — the recipes are the same, just made for more people.

Family sets

Mâm Cơm — built for sharing

A Vietnamese meal is meant to be shared. Our mâm cơm sets bring a full home-style spread to the table, from braised dishes and soup to rice and dessert. Choose a set for two, including our vegan Dưỡng Tâm An Lạc, or gather four for the Mâm Cơm Hội Ngộ reunion platter.

Gathering Meal Set

Dessert: Bánh Flan (caramel custard)
